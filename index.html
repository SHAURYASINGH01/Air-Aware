Prototype transparency

AirAware keeps the Indian CPCB planner separate from a live-data card. When the server has a WAQI token, the card can show a provider-reported station index and its timestamp; otherwise it displays an Open-Meteo model estimate. Neither provider index is copied into the Indian CPCB planner. Saved plans stay in this browser. The app does not send alerts or provide medical advice; verify official guidance before acting.
